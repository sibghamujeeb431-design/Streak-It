## Experiment Summaries

### Gram Staining
Gram staining separates bacteria into Gram-positive (thick peptidoglycan, retains crystal violet → purple) and Gram-negative (thin peptidoglycan + outer membrane, loses crystal violet → pink). It does NOT identify exact species. **Key misconception**: Over-decolorization can make Gram-positive appear falsely Gram-negative; under-decolorization can make Gram-negative appear falsely Gram-positive. Sequence matters, and decolorization is the critical step.

### Streak Plate Method
Streak plating isolates individual colonies from a mixed culture by progressively spreading cells across quadrants. **Key misconception**: One colony does NOT automatically mean one species — isolated colonies are useful but may still contain multiple organisms. Heavy carryover between regions fails to separate colonies properly. Flaming the loop between regions is essential.

### Antimicrobial Susceptibility Test (AST)
AST measures whether bacteria are inhibited by antibiotics using disc diffusion. **Key misconception**: A larger inhibition zone does NOT mean a stronger antibiotic — zone size reflects drug diffusion properties and organism susceptibility, not drug strength. Interpretation must use standardized breakpoints for each specific drug, not raw zone comparisons.

### Serial Dilution & Pour Plate
Serial dilution progressively reduces bacterial concentration across tubes (10^-1 to 10^-5) for viable cell counting. **Key misconception**: Plating the wrong dilution (too concentrated or too dilute) makes colonies uncountable. The ideal plate has 30–300 colonies. CFU/mL calculation requires applying the dilution factor correctly.

### Biochemical Testing
Biochemical testing identifies bacteria by detecting enzymes/metabolic pathways across multiple tests (Catalase, Oxidase, Indole, Citrate, Urease). **Key misconception**: You CANNOT identify a species from a single positive test result — identification requires the full combination of results across all tests. The pattern matters, not any single test.
